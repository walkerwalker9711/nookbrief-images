DNA registry connects Vietnam veteran with the daughter he never knew he had. KTTC's Linda Ha reports.
(KTTC) A woman from Rochester, Minnesota was able to find her father, who she had never met, by using a DNA test kit.
Born in South Vietnam decades ago, Lan Do was ecstatic to meet him for the first time Wednesday when he arrived at Rochester International Airport.
As a young girl, Lan never imagined she would find her long lost family.
But in February, Lan's Mayo Clinic colleague convinced her to submit genetic information to genealogy website ancestry.com.
Six weeks later, she matched to a man named Wayne Brown of Los Angeles.
Lan sent a message to her biological father on Facebook.
Eventually when the pair connected, they took a DNA test, which confirmed the match.

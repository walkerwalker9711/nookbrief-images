CUMBERLAND, Md. (TND) — In a story of incredible kindness and goodwill, people have united to donate over $125,000 to an 82-year-old man after they discovered he was working 40-hour weeks, sometimes more, at Walmart.
Warren "Butch" Marion from Cumberland, Maryland is a veteran of the Navy and a widower, and his work ethic apparently caught the eye of business owner and popular TikTok user Rory McCarty.
McCarty, who owns an extermination business "Bug Boys" and runs a related TikTok account with over 300,000 followers, started up a GoFundMe account for Mr. Marion after meeting him.
But before he did that, McCarty posted a video on TikTok featuring Mr. Marion, asking his followers if they'd be open to donating money so that the hardworking Walmart employee could finally retire.
I was astounded seeing this little older man still grinding. Working 8 to 9 hour shifts," McCarty wrote in the GoFundMe description.
I wanted to help this Navy Veteran to live the remainder of his years traveling to see his kids in Florida," McCarty continued. "Get him off his feet for 8 hrs at a time. And do the things he would love to do that he may not be able to for financial reasons." So far, the GoFundMe account has raised $126,925, and it continues to grow. A check was reportedly cut for Mr. Marion and he put in his two weeks at his job, set to retire.
Local news outlets were there on Mr. Marion's last day of work when McCarty and fans handed him a large check while surrounded by balloons and applause. The first word out of Mr. Marion's mouth when he saw everything was reportedly "wow."
Mr. Marion told the Cumberland Times News that he will use the funds to pay off his house and to travel to Florida to see his kids.
All I can tell you is the good Lord has blessed me for what I did in my younger years," Mr. Marion reportedly said at the time. The check given to Mr. Marion on that day in December was just a portion of the earnings from GoFundMe. McCarty promised he is in contact with Mr. Marion and his family and that "every penny will go to Butch" after GoFundMe takes its cut.

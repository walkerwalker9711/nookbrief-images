# Pops Knows script prompt (read by scripts/senior/draft.py every run)

## Character
- Pops: a 70-something American grandpa on a front-porch rocking chair. 2D cartoon. Calls the viewer "kid".
- Talks like a real grandpa telling you something at the kitchen table: short, dry, a little funny. Never preachy.
- To scammers only: "Nice try, pal." Closing "Pops knows." is allowed but not every episode (at most every other one).
- Opens with the fact or the mistake, never with "Hey guys", "Today we're going to", or a question followed by "Here's the thing".

## Weekday pillars
- Mon Scam: one real scam text or call going around now, the one move that stops it, where to report (FTC reportfraud.ftc.gov, forward texts to 7726).
- Tue Benefits: one discount or benefit with who qualifies, the amount, and where to apply (official .gov or company page). No investment, insurance or Medicare plan advice.
- Wed Home tips: one money-saving number (degrees, percent, dollars) from DOE, EPA, utility or manufacturer pages.
- Thu Nostalgia: a "what did it cost in 1975?" quiz. Old price and today's price, both sourced (BLS, EIA, Census).
- Fri Phone: one iPhone or Android setting, with the real menu path (Settings > Accessibility > ...).

## Script shape
- About 35 seconds, 6 to 9 sentences, 75 to 95 words.
- 5 to 7 scenes. Each scene: 1 or 2 spoken sentences, an on-screen caption of 4 words or fewer, an image_prompt.
- Contractions, mixed sentence length, fragments are fine.

## Banned (AI tone)
delve, dive into, unlock, elevate, seamless, game-changer, robust, navigate, in today's fast-paced world, whether you're X or Y, look no further, it's worth noting, in conclusion, ultimately, must-have, hack your life. No groups of three adjectives, no em dashes, no made-up stories about "my neighbor Linda", no "I tested".

## Pictures (image_prompt)
- 2D flat cartoon, warm small-town America (porch, diner, kitchen, mailbox). Use "Pops" for the character; his look comes from the reference image.
- Phone screens show blank bubbles and icons only. No text, numbers, logos or brand names anywhere in the picture.
- Vertical framing, subject centered, keep the bottom third calm for captions.

## ko_review
Write the whole script again in natural Korean (so the owner can check it), one line per scene.
